Eclipse — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Eclipse ("we", "our", or "the Service") collects, stores, and uses information when users interact with the Eclipse Discord bot and its Minecraft integration.
1. Information We Collect

To provide account-linking functionality, Eclipse may collect and store:

    Discord User ID — to identify Discord accounts.

    Minecraft UUID — to identify Minecraft accounts.

    Minecraft Username — to associate Minecraft accounts with Discord accounts.

    Account linking status — to determine whether accounts are linked.

We only collect information necessary to provide the Service.
2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

    Link Discord accounts to Minecraft accounts.

    Verify account associations.

    Manage account linking and unlinking.

    Prevent unauthorized account linking.

    Maintain and operate Eclipse services.

We do not use this information for advertising or sell it to third parties.
3. Data Storage

Eclipse stores account-linking information in an SQLite database hosted on the infrastructure used to operate the Service.

Access to this database is restricted to authorized administrators and personnel responsible for maintaining Eclipse.
4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' account information.

Information may be accessible to the infrastructure provider hosting the Service as necessary for server operation and maintenance.

We do not intentionally share account-linking information with unrelated third parties.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion

When a user unlinks their Minecraft and Discord accounts, the primary account-linking record is deleted from the active account-linking database.

However, certain associated settings, such as player preferences, language preferences, or administrative bypass settings, may remain stored separately.

Additionally, unlinking events may be recorded in the Discord server's administrative log channel. These records may contain Minecraft usernames, Minecraft UUIDs, Discord User IDs, and information about who performed the action.

Users may contact the Eclipse administration team through the official Discord support server to request the deletion of remaining personal information.

Backup copies and previously generated administrative logs may retain information until they are independently deleted or replaced.

Please note that backup copies may temporarily retain information until they are replaced or deleted.
6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.
7. Third-Party Services

Eclipse operates through Discord and Minecraft-related services. Their respective platforms have their own privacy policies and terms.

Eclipse is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.
8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
9. Contact

For questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests, please contact the Eclipse administration team through our official Discord support server.
